## Different field representation

The selected compact decoder and its cofactor scan remain as previously checked.
The new `cubic_inverse_product_kernel.py` replaces only the root-gated parity of
an inverse product. Its field representation is

    GF(4096) = GF(16)[t] / (t^3-kappa), kappa=2,
    x = a+b*t+c*t^2,
    Q = a^2+kappa*b*c,
    N = a*Q+kappa*b^3+kappa^2*c^3,
    Trace_12(1/x) = Trace_4(Q/N).

The Kummer basis and its inverse are independently generated by finite-field
arithmetic. Zero inverse is defined as zero. `cubic_trace_field.py` checks this
identity on all4096native field values, the small inverter on all16values,
and the four-AND cube decomposition on all16values.

The four-bit inversion core uses five ANDs. Its free input/output maps to the
native GF16 basis were independently found and exhaustively checked. Background
and attribution:
[NIST Circuit Complexity](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/circuit-complexity),
[Boyar, Find and Peralta, Small Low-Depth Circuits (2018)](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=924490).
The cube's four-AND cover is obtained locally from its quadratic polar space.
No claim of optimality for the complete12-bit phase function is needed.

## Runtime classical mask

For each runtime MBUC mask M, the trace pairing gives a unique classical k with
`M dot y = Trace_12(k*y)`. For k nonzero, scale the quantum divisor by1/k using
classically controlled CNOTs. The classical inverse uses ordinary classical
bit operations; no input syndrome is measured. The scaling and its inverse
restore the quantum operands and the original mask exactly. Mask zero skips
the phase. This removes the variable mask from the nonlinear quantum kernel.

The implementation computes Q with9ANDs plus free squaring, N with9ANDs and
two four-AND cubes, and the GF16 inverse with5ANDs. The root-gated parity costs
4ANDs and11root-test gates. Thus the gated inverse costs46Toffolis; together
with the42-Toffoli field product it costs **88**, down from92. It uses78qubits
including36public quantum operands, versus80before. Internal ANDs needed for
inverse cleanup stay live until the output is measured and repaired. Quadratic
norm terms have direct, Toffoli-free measured phase repair.
